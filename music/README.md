# Alien-Themed Background Music

This folder contains phase-specific background music for the Mission Code: Space Hackers game.

## Required Music Files:

### 🏠 **Home & Navigation**
- `home-ambient.mp3` - Mysterious space ambient for home screen
- `phase-selection.mp3` - Epic space exploration theme for phase selection

### 🌌 **C Galaxy (Levels 1-15)**
- `c-galaxy-mysterious.mp3` - Mysterious and robotic soundtrack
  - Style: Dark ambient, computer-like beeps, alien technology sounds
  - Mood: Exploring an alien computer system
  - Tempo: Slow to medium, atmospheric

### 🚀 **C++ Galaxy (Levels 16-30)**  
- `cpp-galaxy-battle.mp3` - Intense space battle music
  - Style: High-energy electronic, futuristic combat sounds
  - Mood: Space battle with alien forces
  - Tempo: Fast-paced, adrenaline-pumping

### 🤖 **Java Galaxy (Levels 31-45)**
- `java-galaxy-uplifting.mp3` - Uplifting sci-fi tech music
  - Style: Bright electronic, digital harmonies, tech sounds
  - Mood: Advanced alien digital kingdom
  - Tempo: Medium to upbeat, inspiring

### 🏆 **Victory**
- `victory-celebration.mp3` - Triumphant space victory theme

## Music Specifications:
- **Format**: MP3 (for broad browser compatibility)
- **Quality**: 128-192 kbps (balance of quality and file size)
- **Length**: 2-4 minutes (seamless looping)
- **Volume**: Normalized to prevent sudden volume changes
- **Looping**: Designed for seamless loop points

## Implementation Notes:
- All tracks automatically loop for continuous gameplay
- Smooth fade transitions between different phases
- Volume controlled by in-game settings (0-100%)
- Mute functionality preserves volume settings
- Tracks change automatically based on current game phase

## Recommended Music Sources:
- Freesound.org (CC licensed)
- Zapsplat (with subscription)
- Adobe Stock Audio
- Custom composition tools (FL Studio, Ableton, etc.)
